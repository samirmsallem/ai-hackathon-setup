# OpenSpec

Lightweight spec-driven flow: propose a change with specs and tasks, apply it, archive it.

## Fit for this challenge
Good for Spec-driven and Minimalist-plus-spec combinations; light overhead.

## Install
See `frameworks/catalog.yaml` entry `openspec`. Always confirm with the team before running installers.

## Reduced path for 120 minutes
One change proposal for the whole prototype, or two (core, UI).

## Risks
Teams may over-split into many small changes; keep to at most three.

## What to log
Framework setup time, any interventions caused by the framework (e.g. dead-end prompts), and any commands you skipped.
