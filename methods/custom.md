# Custom

**Idea:** Define your own method in five lines or fewer; the agent turns it into a runnable workflow.

## Workflow
1. Describe: phases, who does what, checkpoints, validation.
2. The agent restates it as a numbered workflow and a risk list.
3. Confirm and run.

## Suggested agents / skills
Your choice.

## Checkpoints (for Supervised autonomy)
As you define.

## Watch out for
Methods that are only a tool choice. A method describes *how work flows*.

## Document in METHOD_LOG
What you changed from this recipe and why.
